About

What is BRAIN?

The Baton Rouge Analytics & Intelligence Network has been connecting local technology professionals for years. We're a free, community-run group focused on data analytics, business intelligence, machine learning, and AI.

Meetings feature presentations from local experts and Microsoft MVPs, followed by open discussion and networking. Whether you're a seasoned data scientist, a BI developer, or just getting started — you're welcome here.
